# Myths and recycled numbers

Don't state any of these as fact in output. If one matters to the user, quote it as disputed.

- **"Apple indexes screenshot captions (OCR)."** Unproven. The only controlled test found no strong evidence (64 phrases, 1 unexplained). Apple's docs don't list screenshots, and Apple says its screenshot use is AI tagging. [data](https://consultmyapp.squarespace.com/blog/-is-apple-now-indexing-screenshot-titles-on-the-app-store) [official](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/search/) [practitioner](https://techcrunch.com/?p=3018815) · 2026-11-19
- **"Apple denied screenshot indexing."** No primary source exists. [practitioner](https://phiture.com/blog/aso-trends-in-2026/) · 2026-11-19
- **"Ideal Play keyword density is X%."** Google gives no figure, and vendors range from 1% to 5%. [practitioner](https://www.apptweak.com/en/aso-blog/app-store-description-best-practices) [practitioner](https://asomobile.net/en/blog/app-listings-in-google-play-2026/) · 2026-11-19
- **"Repeating a keyword across fields boosts it."** On iOS, repeats are ignored. On Play it is contested. [official](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/search/) [practitioner](https://www.apptweak.com/en/aso-blog/app-store-product-page-optimization) · 2026-11-19
- **"App Store Connect shows keyword data."** It doesn't; the source of the claim retracted it. [official](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/8010/) [practitioner](https://appdrift.co/blog/app-store-connect-keyword-analytics) · 2026-11-19
- **"en-GB keywords count in the US."** Not in any source. [official](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-store-localizations) · 2026-11-19
- **"Autocomplete position = search volume."** Undocumented; use it to show demand exists, nothing more. [practitioner](https://asodesk.com/blog/new-search-suggestions-on-the-app-store-for-the-week/) · 2026-11-19
- **"Search Popularity is stable."** It collapsed in Sep 2025 and changed distribution in Oct 2026. [data](https://aso.dev/blog/apple-ads-popularity-recovery/) · 2026-11-19
- **"Popularity > 25 / KD < 30 is the target."** These are tool-specific scales. [practitioner](https://foxdata.com/en/blogs/app-store-keyword-research-framework-a-datadriven-approach-for-aso-teams-in-2026/) · 2026-11-19
- **"65% of App Store downloads come from search."** This comes from 2016–2018 data. [practitioner](https://sensortower.com/blog/app-store-download-sources) · 2026-11-19
- **"Localization lifts downloads 38% / 128%."** There is no dated primary study behind it. [practitioner](https://www.apptweak.com/en/aso-blog/guide-to-app-store-localization) · 2026-11-19
- **"60% never scroll past the first screenshot."** This is old StoreMaven data quoted second-hand. [practitioner](https://appfollow.io/blog/aso-screenshots-best-practices) · 2026-11-19
- **"Average conversion is 25% (iOS) / 27% (Play)."** These are recycled H1-2024 figures with mixed denominators. Use App Store Connect peer-group benchmarks instead. [practitioner](https://appfollow.io/blog/aso-benchmarks) [official](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect-analytics/benchmarks/peer-group-benchmarks/) · 2026-11-19
- **"Google Play title is 50 characters."** It has been 30 since 2021. [official](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9859152) · 2026-11-19
- **"Google Play indexes What's New."** Unverified. [practitioner](https://www.releasepad.io/blog/mobile-app-release-notes) · 2026-11-19
- **"The July 2026 mass deindexing was an algorithm update."** Only monitoring tools reported it; Apple hasn't confirmed it. [practitioner](https://www.moburst.com/blog/app-store-algorithm-changes-what-changed-and-how-to-adapt/) · 2026-11-19
- **"Tag and screenshot alignment is a ranking signal."** No source is given for this claim. [practitioner](https://phiture.com/blog/aso-stack-redux-2026-article/) · 2026-11-19
