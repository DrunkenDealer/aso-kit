# Habitly

**Positioning:** Habitly is a habit tracker that helps busy people build routines that last.
**Main feature:** a daily checklist of habits with streaks and a weekly review.
**Audience:** busy adults who want a few small routines to stick.
**Stores:** App Store app.habitly.ios · Google Play app.habitly.android
