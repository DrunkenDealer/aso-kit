Grade this App Store and Google Play listing copy. The app's README, its only source of facts:

<readme>
# Habitly

Habitly is a habit tracker for iPhone and Android that helps busy people build routines that last.

- **Today** — the day's habits as a checklist; tap to check one off.
- **Streaks** — a calendar per habit showing every day you kept it.
- **Weekly review** — every Sunday, which habits stuck and which slipped.
- **Reminders** — a nudge at the time you usually do the habit, learned from your check-ins.
- **Widgets** — check off a habit from the home screen.
- **Mood note** — one line about your day, shown next to your habits in the review.

Free with up to 5 habits; Habitly Plus unlocks unlimited habits and the full review history.
</readme>
